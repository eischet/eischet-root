Copy into pom.xml:

    <parent>
        <groupId>com.eischet.root</groupId>
        <artifactId>lib-public</artifactId>
        <version>1.0.8</version>
    </parent>

ArtifactId varies: lib-public, lib-private, app

Apps automatically include the app-tooling jar.
For Beta builds of apps, use the 'beta' profile, which includes an additional jar.
Then, use AppTooling.isBetaVersion to detect. That's a simple way of enabling beta builds, e.g. with additional sanity checks that
you would not want to do in regular builds, e.g. reading all records from a connected database, which we actualy do for some systems.
